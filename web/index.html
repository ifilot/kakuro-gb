How to play

Fill every white cell with a digit from 1 to 9 so that each run of white cells adds up to the sum before it: the upper-right triangle is the sum of the run to its right, the lower-left one of the run below. No digit repeats within a run, and grey cells are given. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

ButtonKeyboardOn the board
D-pad←↑→↓Move to the nearest white cell
AXKeypad: choose a digit, A to put it
BZWipe the cell
SELECTShiftCombinations of the cursor's runs
STARTEnterPause: reveal a cell, start over, music, quit

A gamepad works too. Nothing is saved, just like the cartridge: a puzzle is played in one go, and the best times last until you reload the page.
